# ASW Class Scheduling Rules

> [!NOTE]
> You can schedule your classes up to one hour before each block starts.  
> Read the following instructions carefully to make the most of the scheduling system:

---

## 01 - Class Scheduling

- **System Lock & Unlock Times:** Scheduling locks every day at **0:00 AM** and reopens at **4:40 AM** (America/Bogota timezone), releasing slots for the following day.
- **Next Business Day Slots:** If the system detects that there are no slots available in a selected block, it will automatically enable the next business day for scheduling (via the *"More options"* button).
- **Daily Limit:** You can have up to **5 scheduled classes per day**.
- **Real-Time Availability:** The system refreshes and displays updated availability after each newly scheduled class.

---

## 02 - Cancellations

- **Advance Notice:** Classes can be canceled with a minimum of **24 hours in advance**.
- **Slot Release:** When you cancel on time, your slot is immediately released so another student can take it.
- **Unattended Penalty:** If you fail to cancel on time and do not attend, the system marks the class as taken but unattended (*"vista pero no asistida"*).

---

## 03 - Missed Classes

- If you do not cancel in time and miss the class, you cannot retake it directly from the scheduler.
- To reschedule an unattended class, you must submit a request through the make-up exam/class service (*"servicio de supletorios"*) in the Student Profile on the official ASW website.

---

## 04 - Recommendations

- Consistency in attendance and scheduling is essential for your progress and language proficiency.
- Regularly check availability, as slots update in real time based on other students' cancellations.
